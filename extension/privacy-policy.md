# Privacy Policy — Markup Chrome Extension

**Effective Date:** May 19, 2026

## Overview

Markup ("we", "our", or "us") is a Chrome extension developed with privacy as a foundational principle. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Markup extension.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

Markup does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. All data created within the extension remains on your local device.

## Local Storage

All notes, documents, editor content, theme preferences, and settings are stored exclusively in `chrome.storage.local`, which is a browser-managed storage area local to your device. We do not have access to this data.

## Optional Backend Sync

Markup includes an optional setting for a custom backend URL and API key. This feature is **disabled by default** and intended only for users who choose to self-host their own sync server. If you configure a backend URL, the extension will communicate directly with that server at your direction. We do not operate, control, or monitor any such backend.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on the security of your browser and operating system. We recommend keeping your browser up to date.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the extension's settings page and reflected in an updated effective date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the project's repository.

*Markup is open source and respects your right to privacy.*
