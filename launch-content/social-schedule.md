## Engagement Rules (Save These)

- **Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours.** Algorithmic boost + human connection.
- **Quote-tweet praise.** It validates the user and shows social proof.
- **Never argue with criticism.** *"Fair point — adding to the list"* wins every time.
- **Pin your best tweet** to your profile for the full 3 weeks.
- **Use one link per tweet.** The live URL or GitHub repo. Don't split attention.
