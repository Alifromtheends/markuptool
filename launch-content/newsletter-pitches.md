# Markup — Newsletter & Blog Outreach Pitches

*10 newsletters. Personalized angles. Copy-paste ready. Send in batches of 3 to avoid spam fatigue.*

## How to Use This

1. **Batch 1 (Day 2–3):** TLDR, Console, Pointer — highest reach, developer-focused
2. **Batch 2 (Day 4–5):** JavaScript Weekly, Frontend Focus, Hacker Newsletter — weekly digests
3. **Batch 3 (Day 5–7):** CSS-Tricks, Smashing Magazine, Indie Hackers, Ben's Bites — broader or indie-focused

**Template for outreach:**
- Subject line from below
- 3 paragraphs (pitch, why them, ask)
- Link to live product + repo
- Offer: press kit, screenshots, interview availability
