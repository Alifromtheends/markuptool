## Maker Comment (First Comment to Pin)

Hey Product Hunt!

I built Markup because I was tired of markdown editors that needed accounts, subscriptions, or internet connections to work. I wanted something that just opens and works — no npm install, no cloud sync, no tracking pixels.

Markup is a single HTML file. You can save it to your desktop, open it offline, write markdown, pick from 7 themes (Dark, Minimal, Editorial, Matrix, Paper, Ocean, Brutalist), and export a standalone HTML site. That exported file is also self-contained — drop it on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or email it to someone.

Everything lives in your browser. localStorage only. No server, no database, no analytics.

The new themes (Matrix, Paper, Ocean, Brutalist) were designed to cover every vibe — terminal nostalgia, warm editorial, deep ocean glassmorphism, and raw brutalist energy.

Would love your feedback, theme requests, or contributions. The repo is open source and the whole app is ~800 lines.

— Ali
